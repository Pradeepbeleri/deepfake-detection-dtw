# Research verification, licensing and encoder feasibility

Checked 7 November 2026. **Verified** means an accessible source was inspected; **proposed** means a project decision; **unresolved** means it requires further evidence or permission. These notes are not legal advice.

## Paper and implementation

The project identifies DOI [10.1109/THMS.2025.3618409](https://doi.org/10.1109/THMS.2025.3618409) and [arXiv 2311.02733](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.02733). Both hosts and Crossref were blocked by this environment's network policy, so their linkage, publication metadata, final text and version differences could not be independently checked. An independently hosted [2023 arXiv announcement archive](https://github.com/LeeKyungwook/get-arxiv-noti/issues/834) reproduces the abstract and lists the title **AV-Lip-Sync+: Leveraging AV-HuBERT to Exploit Multimodal Inconsistency for Video Deepfake Detection**, by Sahibzada Adil Shahzad, Ammarah Hashmi, Yan-Tsung Peng, Yu Tsao and Hsin-Min Wang. This is secondary evidence, not the paper itself.

The archived abstract describes AV-HuBERT as visual/acoustic feature extractor and a multiscale temporal convolutional network for cross-modal temporal correlation. It also describes an additional transformer-based video model for facial spatial and temporal artifacts beyond the lips. The brief distinguishes AV-Lip-Sync+ and AV-Lip-Sync+ with facial encoding (FE); do not conflate them. The abstract alone does not verify their exact naming, wiring, training, weights, or ablations. A GitHub repository search did not establish an official complete implementation or detector checkpoint; it cannot establish that none exists. Obtain the final paper and inspect author-linked code and licenses before claiming reproduction or exact comparison. The authors' reported results are not results of this project.

## Official AV-HuBERT and usage gate

[Official repository and README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/av_hubert/tree/258fb50e155134eec2c4b49c2ae8de267075fd18) describes self-supervised audio-visual speech learning and links a [pretrained/fine-tuned checkpoint catalogue](https://facebookresearch.github.io/av_hubert/) and Colab demo. The catalogue host is not allowed here: exact checkpoint availability, download paths and separate weight terms remain unverified. Checkpoints are speech representations, not deepfake classifiers. Upstream recommends Python 3.8, [five pinned packages](https://github.com/facebookresearch/av_hubert/blob/258fb50e155134eec2c4b49c2ae8de267075fd18/requirements.txt) and editable Fairseq; its gitlink pins [Fairseq `afc77bdf4bb51453ce76f1572ef2ee6ddcda8eeb`](https://github.com/pytorch/fairseq/tree/afc77bdf4bb51453ce76f1572ef2ee6ddcda8eeb). CPU/GPU compatibility and dependencies have not been tested.

The [AV-HuBERT license](https://github.com/facebookresearch/av_hubert/blob/258fb50e155134eec2c4b49c2ae8de267075fd18/LICENSE) grants rights to reproduce, distribute and create derivatives **“solely for your non-commercial research purposes”**. Section 2 restricts use of the software products, derivatives, incorporating works or data produced by the software for **“any commercial or production purposes”**, **“purposes of surveillance, including any research or development relating to surveillance”**, and **“biometric processing”**, among other restrictions. The grant is personal, non-transferable and non-sublicensable; attribution and other conditions apply. Read the whole license and confirm what terms apply to each checkpoint and downstream artifact.

| Proposed use | Assessment requiring institutional counsel / rights holder |
| --- | --- |
| Academic experiments | Non-commercial research is within the grant only subject to restrictions. Determine whether lip/face analysis for impersonation constitutes biometric processing or surveillance; research status alone does not settle it. |
| Local research demo | Same restrictions apply; determine whether processing participants or showing outputs is covered and whether data, consent, distribution or derivative terms impose additional limits. |
| Public website | Public access is not automatically commercial, but an operational public service may raise production, biometric, surveillance and third-party-use questions. Do not deploy AV-HuBERT pending written review and any necessary rights-holder authorization. |

## Candidates and decision matrix

GPU and complexity assessments below are planning estimates, not measured requirements. A code license does not automatically clear model weights, training data, user data, privacy law or a specific deployment.

| Stack | Reproducibility / scientific fidelity | License and scope | GPU and implementation cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Original AV-HuBERT + paper temporal head; separate FE variant | Highest conceptual fidelity; paper details, detector code/weights and exact FE architecture unresolved; no reproduction claim | AV-HuBERT terms and checkpoint terms block selection pending review | GPU likely needed for practical fine-tuning; old Fairseq stack and paper gaps make setup high complexity |
| [ImageBind](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ImageBind/tree/53680b02d7e37b19b124fa37bae4b6c98c38f5be) | Joint image/audio embedding is not AV-HuBERT or lip-speech temporal pretraining; temporal windowing and validation required; no published-baseline reproduction | [README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ImageBind/blob/53680b02d7e37b19b124fa37bae4b6c98c38f5be/README.md) says code and weights CC-BY-NC 4.0; [LICENSE file](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ImageBind/blob/53680b02d7e37b19b124fa37bae4b6c98c38f5be/LICENSE) is headed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Conflict needs rights-holder clarification; not a clear public-service candidate | Large model; GPU likely useful, requirements unmeasured; medium/high temporal adaptation complexity |
| **Proposed: Whisper tiny audio + independently initialized ResNet-18 mouth-frame encoder** | Speech encoder and visual branch are separate; late fusion, not shared AV-HuBERT. No faithful reproduction. Needs learned cross-modal alignment or separately validated comparable scalar features for DTW | [Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper/tree/86098128c0b4f24f0e2aa2994de830614b474227) README says code **and weights** MIT; [torchvision code](https://github.com/pytorch/vision/blob/main/LICENSE) BSD-3-Clause. Randomly initialized visual weights avoid assuming terms for ImageNet pretrained weights. License review still needed for actual use, dependencies and data | Whisper tiny is 39M parameters; upstream estimates ~1 GB VRAM for transcription, not our pipeline. CPU smoke test plausible, training GPU likely helpful; medium/high engineering and evaluation complexity |
| Whisper tiny + pretrained torchvision ResNet-18 | Potentially faster visual convergence; still separate encoders and no faithful reproduction | torchvision [warns pretrained models may have separate terms](https://github.com/pytorch/vision#pre-trained-model-license); do not assume ImageNet weights cleared. Review exact weight and data terms first | Upstream [ResNet-18 metadata](https://github.com/pytorch/vision/blob/main/torchvision/models/resnet.py) lists 11.7M parameters and 44.7 MiB weights; GPU helpful for training; medium complexity |

**Conditional recommendation:** Whisper tiny plus randomly initialized ResNet-18 is the preferred fallback for an independent baseline, pending institutional review and verification of all dependencies and the intended deployment. It is a *two-encoder system*, not a single pretrained audio-visual encoder. Whisper has no video-only or joint AV mode, and ResNet has no audio mode. This preserves the scientific question, not the published feature space. Untrained ResNet features are a wiring test only, not a useful visual representation or classifier. An alternative is to use validated, permission-cleared non-neural acoustic energy and mouth-aperture time series for alignment research; these too require demonstrating that their temporal relation is informative.

## Dataset access

- [FakeAVCeleb official repository](https://github.com/DASH-Lab/FakeAVCeleb/tree/0d8d22a44e1750dd2885c5741d7b0a7796304a99): maintainers require a request form and acceptance before sending a download script; it describes synthetic audio and video. Actual agreement, allowed demonstrations/redistribution, subject rights and processing permissions are not established by the README. Obtain and review terms before downloading.
- **DeepfakeTIMIT:** the archived AV-Lip-Sync+ abstract identifies it as an evaluation dataset. We could not verify an official current access mechanism, license, VidTIMIT source-video terms, consent, or audio provenance. A [third-party project](https://github.com/blaketran0117/deepfake-video-detection) describes paired face swaps and VidTIMIT originals, but does not establish rights. Treat access and permissions as unresolved; trace the official provider and review both sources before use.
- [FaceForensics++ official README](https://github.com/ondyari/FaceForensics): application and acceptance are required; data has separate linked terms, distinct from MIT code. Current terms, public demonstration rights, audio content and consent remain to be checked. [DFDC](https://ai.meta.com/datasets/dfdc/) and other datasets remain candidates, not cleared alternatives. No dataset was downloaded.

## Pretrained visual alternatives, verified during real-media validation

Checked 7 November 2026. Torchvision's [pretrained-model notice](https://github.com/pytorch/vision/blob/v0.20.1/README.md#pre-trained-model-license) says individual pretrained models **may** have terms derived from training data; it does not identify a specific ban on non-commercial research for ResNet-18 ImageNet1K V1. Nor does that notice establish a separate affirmative permission for this exact checkpoint. No explicit institutional authorization for this checkpoint has been provided, and the specific artifact's terms remain unresolved. This is a provenance/permission uncertainty, not an asserted prohibition. The supplied file exceeded the environment transfer limit and was not loaded.

A candidate with clearer upstream terms is the ordinary (not X-ray or cell) [DINOv2 ViT-S/14 backbone](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2/tree/7764ea0f912e53c92e82eb78a2a1631e92725fc8): the [upstream README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2/blob/7764ea0f912e53c92e82eb78a2a1631e92725fc8/README.md) explicitly releases DINOv2 code and model weights under Apache 2.0 and links its 21M-parameter pretrained backbone. The separately listed X-ray and cell models have different, more restrictive terms and are not this recommendation. The official backbone download host returned proxy 403; its weights have not been downloaded, hashed or tested. The Apache grant is a practical basis for evaluating the ordinary backbone, subject to the license conditions, any third-party data rights, privacy, consent and deployment review; it does not establish suitability or performance. It is a generic visual image encoder, not a pretrained lip-speech or joint audio-visual model. Its interface and CPU memory/latency need testing before selection for a live demo.

[Google Big Vision](https://github.com/google-research/big_vision#license) also says its codebase, including models, is Apache 2.0 unless noted otherwise; a specific speech-suitable checkpoint and all its terms remain to be checked. [OpenAI CLIP's model card](https://github.com/openai/CLIP/blob/main/model-card.md) describes deployed use and surveillance/facial recognition as out-of-scope; its generic representations and guidance make it a weaker fit for this demonstration. Do not infer a weight license from a code license alone. Recommended fallback is to evaluate the ordinary DINOv2 ViT-S/14 official checkpoint after authorized access, against a simple landmark-motion baseline, while separately validating cross-modal correspondence. This remains an independent baseline, not AV-Lip-Sync+ reproduction.
